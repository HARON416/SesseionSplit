Browser logos bundled for identifying the selected browser on profile cards.
Downloaded 2026-11-09. Images are unmodified 64px PNG exports, rendered at 32px.
No network requests are made to fetch logos while the application runs.

Chrome, Chromium, Brave, Edge, Vivaldi, Opera, Opera GX, and Firefox:
https://github.com/alrra/browser-logos
Source pattern: src/<browser>/<browser>_64x64.png
Local pattern: browser-<browser>.png
The collection's notice is included in BROWSER-LOGOS-LICENSE.txt.

Ungoogled Chromium uses the upstream Chromium logo.
